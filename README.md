
# 🌿 GrowLog
 
**Plant care guides, shared by gardeners.** 🌱
Unleash your inner botanist. Browse care tips for succulents, herbs, flowers and more, then add your own plants to the collection!
 
This is the **frontend** of GrowLog, built with React. It talks to the GrowLog API (see the backend repo).

## ✨ What you can do
 
- 🪴 Browse every plant with its picture, category, care needs and instructions
- ➕ Add a new plant and pick its author and category
- 🧑‍🌾 Add a new gardener (usernames are unique)
- 🎨 Spot each category by its own colour
- 📱 Use it on your phone, tablet or laptop
